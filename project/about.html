Our story

Cooking should fit your life, not compete with it

Mind Your Recipe started with a simple frustration: most recipe sites assume you have an open evening, a full pantry, and no dietary restrictions to work around. We built the opposite — a catalog that starts with your constraints, whether that's 20 minutes, a vegan guest list, or an ingredient you're avoiding, and works forward from there.

Every recipe here is tagged by real prep time, diet, and difficulty so you can filter down to what actually fits tonight, not just what looks good in a photo.

Cooking philosophy

What guides every recipe we publish

Time is an ingredient

Every recipe lists an honest prep time, not an optimistic one. If it says 15 minutes, it takes 15 minutes.

Diets are not an afterthought

Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options are built in from the start, not swapped in as a footnote.

Simple tools, real flavor

You shouldn't need specialty equipment or a pantry of rare spices to eat well on a weeknight.
