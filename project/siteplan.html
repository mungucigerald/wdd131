Mind Your Recipe - Site Plan

Site Name

Mind Your Recipe

The name is a twist from the phrase "mind your own business," turned into an invitation to mind/pay attention to the recipe can actually cook based on the available time and diet you follow. It gives an angle of personalization to the meals.

Site Purpose

This interactive food guide and meal-planning site builds around recipes that are quick, customizable, and healthy. Visitors can access a browsable and filterable catalog of recipes suites to the available time and dietary needs, also an option to submit their own recipe ideas as well as feedback to the site

Scenarios

1. What is the best meal I can prepare for some vegeterian guests in 20minutes?

2. I need to find a recipe with a short cook time before I commit to read one!

3. How can share this recipe for a week night to food enthusiasts?

Pages

Home

Brief introduction, hero banner, search bar, and featured highlight recipe, entry points into the Explorer and Submission pages.

Recipe Explorer

Full recipe catalog as a responsive card grid built from a JavaScript array of recipe object and with filters ie cuisine, meal type, diet, prep time among others and a search bar

Submit a Recipe / Feedback

A form for visitors to submit their own recipe ideas and leave feedback on a recipe they tried with a client side validation and confirmation message.

References

Unstyled citation page for any outside sources or images, linked from the footer or all pages.
